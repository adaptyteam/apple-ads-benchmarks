# Required behavior fixtures

These examples define answer behavior, not exact prose.

## PDF Reader CR

**Prompt:** "What is the conversion rate benchmark for PDF Reader?"

Return the 77.9% sub-niche CR and define it as downloads/taps on the App Store product page. Do not
describe it as install-to-trial or trial-to-paid conversion.

## Utilities in Brazil

**Prompt:** "What is the Utilities benchmark in Brazil?"

State that no combined Utilities-in-Brazil row exists. Either stop there or show the global
Utilities aggregate and all-category Brazil aggregate as two independently labeled marginals. Do
not produce a single blended benchmark or verdict.

## Weekly trial-to-paid

**Prompt:** "What is the weekly-trial to paid benchmark?"

Say that a matching trial-to-paid baseline is not published. Do not substitute install-to-paid,
install-to-trial, or a category median from another funnel step.

## CPI

**Prompt:** "What is the Apple Ads CPI benchmark?"

Ask whether CPI means spend/downloads or spend/installs. Explain that this report publishes CPA =
spend/downloads and does not publish CPI = spend/installs. Do not silently return CPA under a CPI
label.
